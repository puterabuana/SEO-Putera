01 / Overview

Content and crawlable structure became visible to the audit tools.

Liemoro is a yoga studio website focused on vinyasa, breathwork, yin yoga, and yoga nidra.

The paired reports document a homepage that changed from a thin, nearly linkless page into a fuller page with an explicit heading structure, substantial copy, internal navigation, a stronger search title and description, and richer social metadata.

Evidence disclosure

All four paired reports are supplied files dated 16 Aug 2026. They verify automated on-page and technical checks only; they do not establish higher rankings, visitor growth, bookings, or business results.

0165→89

Rank Math passed checks rose from 19 of 28 to 25 of 28, with failed checks down from six to two.

0267%→84%

Seobility recorded richer page quality and link structure, with 1,047 words and 19 internal links in the later run.

0384→89

SEO Site Checkup improved by five points while failed checks fell from ten to six.

04C+→B

SEOptimer rose one grade and reduced its recommendation count from 22 to 20.

02 / What changed

Core on-page signals were added and connected.

01

Heading structure was added

The baseline reports flagged no H1 and no H2 headings. The later reports detect one H1 and multiple H2 sections.

Content hierarchy
02

Useful page content expanded

Seobility moved from zero detected words to 1,047 words, giving search engines more crawlable context for the studio.

On-page content
03

Internal navigation became crawlable

The later Seobility report lists 19 internal links where the baseline had none, improving route discovery and link structure.

Internal linking
04

Search snippets were strengthened

The title expanded from 31 to 53 characters and the description from 108 to 146 characters in the source reports.

Metadata
05

Social context was improved

The later reports record Open Graph and Twitter image metadata alongside an updated description and canonical URL.

Sharing metadata
06

Remaining work stays visible

The latest reports still identify issues such as H1 review, links, analytics, and performance work. This is an improved state, not a finished programme.

04 / Limitations

What the score changes do not prove.

01

No ranking or booking claim

The reports verify automated checklist changes, not higher organic rankings, visitor growth, class bookings, or revenue.

02

One current PageSpeed run

Google PageSpeed Insights was run separately after the supplied audit set: mobile scored 88/100 and desktop scored 99/100. No baseline PageSpeed run is claimed.

03

Links remain an open issue

SEOptimer still grades links F. The stronger internal-link signal does not establish off-page authority.

04

Some issues remain

The later reports still list six failed Site Checkup checks and 20 SEOptimer recommendations. The work is improved, not presented as complete.
