01 / Overview

Clearer crawlable signals, validated by separate tools.

Biotek Farmasi is a supplement brand website with product, ingredient, and purchase-oriented content.

The paired reports document changes to the homepage's technical and on-page signals: canonical control, heading structure, metadata, internal links, structured data, language, and sitemap discovery. The report results differ in scope, so this page treats each score as tool-specific evidence—not as one averaged metric.

Evidence disclosure

The baseline reports were generated on 15 Aug 2026 and the later reports on 16 Aug 2026. Google PageSpeed Insights was separately captured on mobile, returning 93/100; its desktop run returned no score, and no before PageSpeed report exists. The study makes no claim about rankings, traffic, sales, or medical efficacy.

0166→98

Rank Math passed tests increased from 18 of 28 to 27 of 28, with failed checks down from seven to one.

0258%→100%

Seobility reached a 100% SEO score, including 100% page structure and link structure.

0366→82

SEO Site Checkup increased by 16 points while failed checks fell from 17 to 11.

04C→B

SEOptimer rose one grade and its recommendation count fell from 22 to 15.

02 / What changed

Foundational signals were made explicit.

01

Canonical URL was added

The baseline checks could not find a canonical URL. The later Rank Math and Seobility reports detect a valid canonical for the homepage.

Canonicalisation
02

Headings and copy became crawlable

The later audit detects one H1, multiple H2s, 283 words, and internal links—signals that were missing or inconsistent in the baseline reports.

Content structure
03

Metadata became more descriptive

The title changed from 45 to 55 characters and the description from 76 to 143 characters, while both later reports recognise the intended product context.

On-page SEO
04

Link structure was established

Seobility moved from zero detected links to 15 internal and one external link, with link structure reported at 100%.

Internal linking
05

Structured context was reviewed

Baseline reports listed schema and sitemap gaps. The later reports show fewer remaining technical issues, but residual warnings stay visible in the source files.

Schema · sitemap
06

Remaining work is still visible

Links remain graded F in SEOptimer, while performance and off-page recommendations continue. The score rise is not presented as a finished SEO programme.

04 / Limitations

What the score changes do not prove.

01

No ranking or sales claim

The reports verify automated checklist changes, not higher organic rankings, more visitors, sales, or product outcomes.

02

No PageSpeed comparison

A mobile result of 93/100 is captured, but no before result exists and the desktop run returned no score. It is shown as a current snapshot, not a performance gain.

03

Links remain an open issue

SEOptimer still grades links F. The on-page and technical gains do not establish off-page authority.

04

Some issues remain

The later Site Checkup report still lists 11 issues and SEOptimer retains 15 recommendations. The work is improved, not presented as complete.
